2. What data we process

When you visit the website

Your browser necessarily sends your IP address and the address you request. We keep no access log: neither the web server nor the application stores IP addresses. Only to stop password guessing, the application remembers failed logins per IP address for 15 minutes in memory (never on disk). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (security of the service).

Cookie

We set exactly one cookie (ec_session), from your first visit on and also without logging in. It keeps you logged in, remembers your language and protects forms against cross-site requests (CSRF). It is signed, expires after 30 days and is strictly necessary for the service (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG), so we don't ask for consent. There are no tracking or advertising cookies.

Your account

  • your email address and a hash of your password (Argon2id; we never know the password itself),
  • your text from the registration (“Who are you?”), so we can approve registrations by hand,
  • settings: language, time zone, eco/price priority, charge durations and rules like “ready by 7:00”.

Your plug and your charges

  • the access token of your Meross account, encrypted (AES-GCM), plus your Meross account's email, which Meross returns with the token,
  • the name and ID of your plugs,
  • the plug's power readings (about every 30 seconds) and its on/off state,
  • your charges: plug-in time, planned and actual charging, energy, computed savings, button presses on the plug.

This data shows when you plug in and charge your device. We use it only to plan your charging, learn how long a charge takes and show you what you saved. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the service you use).

Demo

The demo uses a simulated plug. It creates an anonymous demo account without an email, which is deleted with all its data two hours after its last use.

3. Meross

Efficharge controls Meross plugs through the Meross cloud (Chengdu Meross Technology Co., Ltd., China). Efficharge is not made by or affiliated with Meross; it uses the unofficial open-source library meross_iot.

  • Login: When you connect your Meross account, our server sends your Meross email, password and, if used, the two-factor code to Meross once, to get an access token. The password is then discarded and never stored or logged.
  • Operation: With the token, our server keeps a connection to the Meross cloud. Through it, it reads your plug's power, switches it on and off and learns when you press its button. Meross thereby sees that your account is used from our server, and the switch commands and queries. We pass no other data about you to Meross.
  • Where: Efficharge uses Meross's European server (iotx-eu.meross.com). Meross itself is responsible for its own processing, including any transfer to third countries such as China; the Meross privacy policy applies, which you agreed to when you set up your Meross account.
  • Disconnect: In Settings → Plugs you remove the Meross account; the token and all data of its plugs are then deleted. If you also change your Meross password, the token becomes invalid at Meross too.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; the connection to Meross is needed for Efficharge to switch your plug.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account, settings and charges: until you delete your account (Settings → Account → Delete account). Everything is then removed from the database at once. A registration that isn't approved is deleted.
  • Power readings outside of charges: 90 days.
  • Meross token and plug data: until you remove the Meross account in the settings. You remove a single plug in its own settings; its charges and readings are deleted then.
  • Database backups, so nothing is lost if the server fails: at most {{ imprint.backup_days }} days, including the copies kept off the server. Deleted accounts disappear from these copies as they expire; if we ever restored one, we would delete them again.

6. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) (Art. 21). You can delete your account with all its data yourself at any time, in Settings → Account. For anything else, just send an email {% if imprint.has_imprint %}to {{ imprint.imprint_email }}{% else %}to the operator{% endif %}.

You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one where you live.

7. Security

The connection is encrypted with HTTPS. Access tokens are stored encrypted, passwords only as a hash. The website loads no scripts, fonts or images from other servers. The source code is public.
